XenoLog roster check

Compares every frog photo with every other one and lists where the photos and the IDs disagree: photos filed under one ID that do not match each other, and photos filed under different IDs that do. It uses the same pattern check as the app, so it is a suggestion; look at the photos before changing anything. The files you choose are read in this browser and are not sent anywhere. The pattern check (OpenCV.js) is downloaded from jsDelivr the first time.

1. Photos

In the spreadsheet, open the Photos sheet and choose File > Download > Comma-separated values. Do the same for the Frogs sheet (optional: it limits the check to frogs in the colony and lists frogs with no photo). Choose both files here. The backs are compared as they were cropped in the app.

Image files whose names start with the frog ID, such as 125.jpg, 125 back.png, or the app's own frog-125_2026-10-14_P1a2b3c4d.jpg. The back is found automatically, which misses on some photos.
